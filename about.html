RASHANI'S PRACTICE

A path
that keeps
opening.

Born into a family of artists, Rashani began writing and studying sumi-e painting at eight. Her biography traces her engagement with art, civil rights, feminist movements, spiritual teachers, music, and communities of shared practice.

1964

Illustrated poetry published in Contact magazine while she was twelve.

1982

Founded Sacred Spiral Press, beginning a substantial body of published books.

1990

Ordained by Thich Nhat Hanh into the Order of Interbeing.

2002

Received stewardship of the land that became Kipukamaluhia.

Today

Continues the work through art, books, mentoring, and care for the sanctuary on Hawaiʻi Island.

RELATIONSHIPS THAT SHAPE THE WORK

Teachers,
friends &
living influences.

Rashani's artistic practice is inseparable from the people and teachings that have touched her life. Her homage archive includes figures such as Joanna Macy, Sister Corita, Ram Dass, and Thich Nhat Hanh.

Her recent tribute journal to Joanna Macy brings that relationship into a new artistic form.
